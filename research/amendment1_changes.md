- Revenue +47% q/q, +1,127% y/y (p. 10). Our pre-amendment estimate was ~$16.9B (86 × Q4 2024's $197M).
- Back to a GAAP operating loss: "primarily driven by $5.0 billion of compute under short-term commitments, which
  currently costs significantly more than comparable compute procured under our multi-year commitments, and $2.0 billion
  of non-cash charges related to our donation matching program" (p. 138; p. 162–163).
- Revenue per $1 of compute fell from 2.08× (Q2) to 1.72× (Q3). The company's chart callout changed from "5.2x increase
  in compute efficiency" to "4.3x — >4x compute efficiency gain since Q1'25". Compute was 58% of revenue (48% in Q2) (p. 162).
- "This reduced revenue per dollar of compute compared to the preceding quarter." (p. 137)
- Price cuts: "a decrease of approximately $600 million from price reductions introduced as part of new model versions
  and contractual discounts" between Q2 and Q3 (p. 161). Q4 2025: ~$70M.
- Personnel fell in Q3 because certain option awards became fully vested (p. 162).
- Sales and partnerships fell from 22% (Q1) to 14% (Q3) of revenue because less revenue came through channel partners
  and because of "changes in the terms of our channel partner distribution arrangements" (p. 162).
- Q3 income-tax provision $396M from higher state and foreign taxes (p. 163).
- Our derived Q2→Q3 incremental view: revenue +$5.4B; compute +$4.3B (80¢ of each new dollar); operating income −$1.5B;
  adjusted operating income +$0.2B.

## Filled in: the compute bill (pp. 6, 33, 139, 173–175, 184, 200)
- "aggregate committed spend of approximately **$895.4 billion**" (our footnote reconstruction: ~$855B).
- **$447.5B** expected before December 31, 2030; substantially all the rest 2031–2045.
- **$69.8B** due in the 12 months after September 30, 2026, of which $4.3B is conditional or terminable.
- **$213.8B** "relates to arrangements that are subject to either exercisable termination rights or contingent
  conditions prior to commencement, such as the counterparty's ability to secure financing, facilities, or regulatory approvals".
- Contractual table (Sept 30): total $320.0B ($313.8B purchase commitments, $4.2B operating and $2.0B finance leases),
  by year: Q4 2026 $5.2B, 2027 $29.2B, 2028 $44.4B, 2029 $47.8B, 2030 $45.1B, thereafter $148.4B.
- Plus: short-term minimums $17.4B; leases signed not yet commenced **$451.2B** (June 30: $321.4B), weighted-average term
  ~10 years, commencing 2026–2028; signed after Sept 30: **$100.2B** plus $6.6B short-term.
- Not counted: TPU and other non-fixed arrangements estimated at **$150–175B** (was $125–150B plus >$20B AMD).
- Named deals: Google raised to **≥$121.3B** (from ≥$114.5B), Microsoft ≥$44.9B now to June 2033; Amazon $110B,
  SpaceXAI up to $120.5B, Nscale ~$44.6B, Lambda ~$41.3B, Broadcom leases ~$161.2B unchanged. Broadcom assigned part
  of the 2026 lease to a new special-purpose financing entity in October (p. 176).
- New risk-factor language: compute providers "depend on third-party financing or credit support", and capacity "may be
  delayed, repriced, reduced, or not delivered" if that financing dries up (p. 31).
- New: "we expect an increasing portion of our compute and infrastructure investment to take the form of capital
  expenditures and other commitments … that require significant upfront payments" (p. 165).

## Safety and governance disclosures — new
- p. 29: "our internal estimates and our expectations regarding future models … indicate that we may imminently meet our
  Responsible Scaling Policy's definition of 'dramatically accelerated progress attributable to AI.' We have not
  designated any of our current models as exceeding this threshold".
- p. 52: considering RSP updates "to require human review and approval of certain decisions in our research and
  development processes as our models perform an increasing share of that work."
- p. 188: "we do not anticipate being able to safely use Claude to fully autonomously design and build successive
  generations of frontier models without meaningful human supervision or understanding … we are considering updates to
  our Responsible Scaling Policy to keep humans in control of frontier model development at Anthropic."
- Government revenue now "less than 2%" (was "approximately 1%") (p. 72).
- New product mentions: Claude Ultra plan (p. 2); Claude Duet, Claude Marketplace, Claude for Government (MD&A chart).
- New lawsuit: copyright claim under Canada's Copyright Act (p. F-60).
- Governance, LTBT, Class F and lock-up terms: wording changes only.
