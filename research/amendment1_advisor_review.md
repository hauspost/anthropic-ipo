- **"≈$106B in the next 12 months" does not match the code.** It can be reproduced only with a yearly-bucket shortcut: ¼ of the 2026 bucket plus ¾ of 2027, on the like-for-like basis (10.9 + 95.3 = $106.2B). - **"Overstated the near-term crunch" is fair for year one only.** The full picture: the filed schedule is more back-loaded, and the filed total is $40B larger. Caveats:
  - The $69.8B excludes future short-term purchases. Q3 alone had $5.0B, and the company says use will "continue to increase" (p.135).

It says "paying for 2027's contracted compute ... would take roughly $260 billion of revenue, nearly five times our 2026 estimate". That was 131.1 × 2.0. - Readout and hint text to update:

  - The "$131.7B" cash figure becomes $131.8B at Sept 30. Keep it as cash and short-term investments; do not switch to the $146.6B liquidity figure.
  - The hint "~$16.9B for Q3" becomes the actual $16.96B.
  - The 2026 base of $55B now implies Q4 of $21.8B (+28% q/q). Say so.
- Anchoring advice. Constrain the spread so that it sums to $69.8B for Oct 2026–Sep 2027, $447.5B through 2030 and $895.4B in total. ## 5. Amazon line (B22): the current site text is wrong

- **Current lines.** `content.py` line 47: "Amazon booked almost the same amount as a gain". Line 189: "...while Anthropic booked a $72.4B loss **on the same instruments**". Line 196: "...a $72.4B loss **on the same notes**".
