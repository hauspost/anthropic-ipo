# Anthropic IPO: news coverage map (Dec 2025 to Oct 26, 2026)

Compiled 2026-10-26. This covers news reporting only; expert commentary is handled separately. The structured version is in `news_coverage.json`, which has the same content plus every URL.

**Sourcing conventions**
- Reuters, WSJ, FT, Bloomberg, CNBC, Business Insider, Barron's, MarketWatch and The Verge were paywalled or blocked when fetched directly. Their reporting appears below through syndicated copies or secondary write-ups, and each such item is marked *secondhand* with both outlets named.
- **Gaps.** The web-search budget, which is shared with other agents, ran out before dedicated searches on four topics:
  - retail allocation through Robinhood or SoFi
  - S&P 500 inclusion
  - anchor or cornerstone investors
  - direct coverage from Axios, Semafor, NYT and The Verge

  On these topics I found only what turned up incidentally.
- The DRS amendment dates (Jul 13, Aug 10, Sep 4, Oct 1, Oct 9) come from the brief, which took them from EDGAR. I did not verify them, and I found no news story about any individual amendment. However, the Sep 4 amendment coincides with the FT and Reuters reports that day about lead-left and the delay, and the Oct 1 amendment coincides with Bloomberg's report of a mid-November target.

## 5. Market context

- **SpaceX/xAI:** SpaceX acquired xAI in February. It priced at $135 on Jun 11, raising $75B at a $1.77T valuation, or $86.2B including the greenshoe, which made it the largest IPO on record. It traded near $147 by late September. Musk holds about 82% of the vote (Fortune). Anthropic has agreed to buy up to $120.5B of compute from SpaceX.AI.
- **OpenAI:** raised $122B at $852B in March and filed confidentially on Jun 8. Its listing is now expected in 2027. It is reportedly raising $30B, at $1.4T according to Bloomberg on Sep 29 and at $1.25T pre-money according to CNBC on Oct 22. The WSJ reports that OpenAI missed its user and revenue targets and expects to burn about $85B in 2028.
- **AI-bubble worries:** a mid-October selloff in AI and tech stocks hit CoreWeave's shares and bonds on Oct 15. AP and Reuters attribute the selloff to doubts about debt-fuelled AI spending. The autumn IPO calendar had thinned. Apollo's Torsten Slok said AI has produced "zero financial payoff" outside tech, and Simon Dedic warned of the "top of the AI bubble". Back in Dec 2025, the Bank of England and Michael Burry had issued bubble warnings, which framed the first IPO reports.
- **Market reaction on filing day:** the S&P 500 and Nasdaq were heading for their best day in more than a month, and the SOX chip index rose more than 3% (Reuters). The Nasdaq Composite was up 0.8% at midday (Yahoo). Premarket, Nvidia rose 1.4%, Broadcom 1.5% and Micron 2.3% (CoinDesk). CoreWeave and Nebius reversed early losses. On the same day, Meta said it plans to raise up to $95B in equity.
- **IPO market:** by Aug 19, US IPOs had raised $160.6B in 2026, against the 2021 record of $195.2B (Bloomberg). An Anthropic deal at SpaceX's scale would make 2026 a record year.
