# Anthropic S-1/A (Amendment No. 1): outside coverage, expert reaction and market context

Compiled 2026-11-03. Scope: news and commentary about Amendment No. 1 (accession 0001628280-26-068676, filed 2026-11-02). The filing itself was not read for this file. All figures are as outlets reported them and should be checked against the filing. Machine-readable version: `amendment_commentary.json` in the same folder.

Reliability labels: **direct** means read on the outlet's own page or a wire copy carrying the wire credit. **Syndicated** means read on a partner site under the original byline. **Secondhand** means the original was paywalled or not reached, so the content comes from another outlet's summary (both outlets are named). **Headline only** means only a headline or snippet was seen. **Primary (other)** means read in another company's SEC filing.

## The headline: the amendment has no deal terms

Every outlet that checked says Amendment No. 1 leaves the **price range, the share count and the offering size blank** ([Yahoo Finance/Blikre, Nov 2](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/article/anthropics-new-ipo-filing-puts-its-computing-bill-near-900-billion-151830444.html): "The price range and share count are still blank"; also [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/329931/20261103/anthropic-ipo-filing-shows-revenue-growth-slowing-investors-cut-target-valuation.htm) and [Stocktwits](https://stocktwits.com/news-articles/markets/equity/anthropic-updates-ipo-filing-as-investors-push-for-lower-valuation-discloses-17-b-in-q3-revenue-slowing-growth-and-nearly-900-b-in-commitments/cNGQpAqR95R)). A further amendment with a range is expected around the roadshow launch. The new material is Q3 2026 results, the full commitments total and the named retail platforms. **Every valuation and timing figure now in circulation comes from press reporting (WSJ, Reuters, Bloomberg), not from the filing.**

## 3. Market context

- **The selloff behind the valuation cut.** Alphabet reported on Oct 28 and fell 10.4% after hours. Oct 29 was described as its worst day, costing nearly $500B of market value. Amazon reported on Oct 29 and fell about 6% after hours. On Oct 30 Intel fell 28.5% and the Nasdaq 2.86% ([The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/business/markets/2026/10/30/intel-amzn-nasdaq-ai/)). On Nov 2 the Nasdaq fell another 1.07% to 25,164.36 and the S&P 500 0.51%. **The Nasdaq-100 closed more than 10% below its record, which is a correction** ([Invezz](https://invezz.com/news/2026/11/03/nasdaq-rises-as-chip-stocks-rebound-while-apple-and-palantir-shares-fall/)). The same day Oracle fell 8.8% after announcing a $10B stock sale and a $10B bond sale, and Marvell fell 12.7% on its $115B Lumentum deal. Alphabet priced a €6B bond ([SEC FWP](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1652044/000119312526443857/d134038dfwp.htm)). On Nov 3, midterm election day, chips rebounded intraday (Nvidia +3.3%, AMD +4.6%) and the Nasdaq was up about 0.4–0.8%. That is not a close.
- **Alphabet Q3** ([8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001652044/000165204426000080/googexhibit991q32026.htm), [10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1652044/000165204426000084/goog-20260930.htm)). Revenue $127.6B, EPS $2.12. Other income was **−$8.2B**, driven mainly by an unrealized loss on its **SpaceX** stake. The gain on non-marketable equity was only **$3.39B in Q3**, against $117.2B for the first nine months. Carrying value is $128.2B, "primarily" one unnamed private company. **Alphabet does not name Anthropic.**
- **Amazon Q3** ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/29/amazon-amzn-q3-earnings-report-2026.html), [10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1018724/000101872426000040/amzn-20260930.htm)). EPS $1.89 missed, AWS grew 39%, capex was $77.3B and trailing free cash flow was −$42.4B. **There was essentially no Anthropic income-statement gain in Q3 2026** (all private-equity upward adjustments totaled $39M), against $62.8B for the first nine months. Profit fell year over year partly because Q3 2025 included a $9.5B Anthropic gain. At Sept 30, Amazon held Anthropic preferred worth $92.5B (164M shares, each convertible into 10 common) and notes with a fair value of $146.2B. The $140.3B unrealized gain on the notes sits in AOCI, not net income. Amazon expects a lock-up after the IPO.
- **OpenAI** ([Reuters/FT via Investing.com](https://investing.com/news/economy-news/openai-slashes-funding-target-by-10-billion-amid-tech-rout-ft-reports-oct-30-4990653)). OpenAI cut its round target to about $20B and its valuation to **$1.25T from $1.4T**. It still plans to list in 2027 and wants to close the round ahead of "Anthropic's imminent listing."
- **SpaceX as a comparable.** It priced at $135 on June 11 (about $1.75–1.77T) and closed its first day at $161. It peaked at about $211 (intraday high $225.64), hit a low close of **$108.27 in early August**, and was back above $135 by October. It closed at about **$136.37 on Nov 2**, roughly flat to the IPO price after five months. Nov 3 intraday was $141.65 ([Fool quote](https://www.fool.com/investing/how-to-invest/stocks/how-to-invest-in-spacex-stock/); [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/10/spacex-spcx-stock-ipo-price.html); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Initial_public_offering_of_SpaceX)). It joined the Nasdaq-100 through fast entry 15 trading days after listing.
- **Index inclusion.** The Nasdaq-100 fast-entry rule (15 trading days, with a top-40 size test) should apply to Anthropic. **S&P 500 rules were not relaxed**: they require 12 months of trading and four quarters of positive GAAP earnings, and Anthropic's GAAP losses rule it out for now ([Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/09/23/anthropic-could-join-the-nasdaq-100-only-15-trading-days-after-its-ipo-just-like-spacex-did/), [ETF Stream](https://www.etfstream.com/articles/s-and-p-500-will-not-adopt-fast-entry-for-spacex-anthropic-and-openai)).
- **Credit.** KBRA rated Anthropic BB+ in June. The FT reported on Oct 30 (via [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/30/anthropic-expected-to-receive-high-junk-rating-from-credit-agencies-following-ipo-report-says/)) that Blackstone expects a double-B rating after the IPO.
- **Retail mood.** Stocktwits sentiment was "bearish" late on Nov 2, and Robinhood shares fell 1.4%.

## 4. Where sources disagree

1. **Valuation talk:** WSJ says $1.5–1.6T; Reuters about $1.5T; Bloomberg $1.4–1.5T; Dataconomy $1.4–1.5T, with $1.75T possible (unattributed); Fortune frames it as "seeking $1.5–2T".
2. **Q3 revenue:** $16.96B and +46.7% (Reuters, BeInCrypto); "nearly $17B" and ~47% (CNBC, Fortune); $16.8B and +45% in the Oct 14 investor communication. Stocktwits/Yahoo (Gill) wrote "up from about $11.5B a year earlier", which appears to be an error because the year-ago figure was about $1.4B.
3. **Compute expense:** $9.86B, $9.9B or about $10B (rounding).
4. **Commitments baseline before the amendment:** the user's note of about $855B in footnotes; TechTimes' $265.6B as of June 30; about $800B per WSJ analysts; Yahoo's purchase commitments of $314B (vs. $55B at end-2025). These use different definitions.
5. **Timing:** listing the week of Nov 16 (WSJ, Dataconomy); before Nov 26 (Bloomberg via TechTimes); Reuters gives only "marketing next week". A Stocktwits user speculated about Nov 12; low reliability.
6. **Raise size:** about $100B (WSJ) vs. $75B or more (IPOScoop).
7. **Retail platforms named:** 5 (Yahoo) vs. 6 including Trade-PMR (FourWeekMBA headline).
8. **The "more than 11,900 customers at $100K" figure** is dated Sept 30 by Reuters but attributed to the original June 30 S-1 by IPOScoop and The National.
9. **Nasdaq on Oct 30:** −2.86% (The National, Stocktitan), −2.48% (unidentified report) or "nearly 3%" (Dataconomy).
10. **SpaceX IPO:** raised $75B (base) vs. about $86B (with greenshoe); valued at $1.75T vs. $1.77T; peak $211 vs. $225.64 (intraday); retail split 70:30 (Wikipedia) vs. 15–20% (Stocktwits).
11. **Dan Ives's affiliation:** Yorkville Advisors (Stocktwits), Yorkville Ives & Co. (Bloomberg listing) or Wedbush (older coverage).
